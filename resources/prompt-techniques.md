# Prompt Engineering Notes

## Zero-Shot Prompting

Definition:
Giving the AI a task without providing any examples.

Example:

Summarize this paragraph.

Best Used For

- Translation
- Summarization
- Classification
- Simple questions

---

## One-Shot Prompting

Definition:
Giving one example before asking the AI to perform the task.

Example

Question:
What is AI?

Answer:
Artificial Intelligence is the simulation of human intelligence by machines.

Now answer:

What is Machine Learning?

---

## Few-Shot Prompting

Definition

Giving multiple examples before asking the AI to respond.

Example

Informal:
Thanks.

Professional:
Thank you very much.

Informal:
Send it.

Professional:
Please send it at your earliest convenience.

Informal:
Call me.

Professional:
Could you please call me when you're available?

Now convert:

Informal:
See you tomorrow.

AI Output

I look forward to seeing you tomorrow.

---

## Comparison

| Technique | Examples | Best For |
|-----------|----------|----------|
| Zero-Shot | None | Simple tasks |
| One-Shot | One | Style guidance |
| Few-Shot | Multiple | Consistent formatting |
